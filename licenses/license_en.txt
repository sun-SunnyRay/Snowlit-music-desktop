1.2 This project cannot fetch audio by itself. Playback URLs come from the custom source selected in Settings. The project only sends title, artist, and similar fields to that source. If the source returns a link, the project treats it as the audio for that song and cannot verify that it is correct. Playback may not match the requested track, or may fail.
